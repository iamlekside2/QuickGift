Become a provider

Ready to grow? Join us.

Join the waitlist today. When we launch you will set up your shop and get verified right inside the QuickGift app.

Who can join?

Bakers, florists, nail techs, makeup artists, tailors, photographers, caterers, gift packers, or any gifting or personal-service business in Lagos or Abuja that does great work.

How much does it cost?

Joining is free. Founding providers pay 0% commission at launch, and you'll always see our fees clearly before you accept any order.

How do I get paid?

Customers pay through QuickGift when they place an order, so the money is secured before you start. Your earnings are paid out to your Nigerian bank account.

What happens after I join?

When onboarding opens we reach out on WhatsApp, then you set up your shop and get verified right inside the app, for free.
